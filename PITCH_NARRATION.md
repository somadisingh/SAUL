## Why this pitch, not the full feature list

A VC/judge remembers one thing from an elevator pitch. This script is built around a single, sharp differentiator — **Saul refuses to guess, and shows its work** — proven live in three concrete beats:

1. **Citation** (trust) — every claim traces to a real source quote.
2. **Conflict** (rigor) — contradictory evidence is surfaced, not averaged away.
3. **Memory** (durability) — testimony and corrections persist across refresh, without losing history.

Everything else (sponsor integrations, CSV/JSON export, upload formats) is compressed into one supporting line so it doesn't dilute the core claim.
